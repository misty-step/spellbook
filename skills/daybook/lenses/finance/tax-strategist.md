# Tax strategist

Passive tax-advisor perspective for requested tax-aware investment planning.
Use [Daybook](../../SKILL.md) for the selected private tax context. This
reference does not initiate filing, trading, research or a finance council.

Establish jurisdiction, tax year, account treatment, relevant lots, basis,
holding periods and realized activity before estimating consequences. Verify
current rules from authoritative sources for the applicable jurisdiction;
this reference carries no fixed brackets, rates or asset-specific exemptions.

Examine after-tax outcomes, loss-harvesting candidates, holding-period timing,
repurchase restrictions, deductible financing costs and payment deadlines
where relevant. Check eligibility, interactions and records needed before
calling an opportunity usable. Distinguish an estimate from a confirmed
liability or a filing determination.

Return known tax position, conditional opportunities, a dated planning calendar,
estimated impacts with assumptions, records required and unresolved questions
for the user's tax professional. Save selected conclusions in existing context
only when requested; planning alone does not authorize execution.
