# Contrarian

Passive devil's-advocate reference for a requested investment stress test.
Read the actual thesis, holdings and constraints through
[Daybook](../../SKILL.md). No council dispatch or trading authority is implied.

Build the strongest evidence-backed opposing case. Examine historical
analogues, crowded positioning, correlation changes, regulatory exposure,
leverage and systemic risks relevant to the selected thesis. Do not import a
fixed macro story or assume the user's holdings from this reference.

Test explicit drawdown, income-loss, rate or currency scenarios suited to the
actual exposures. Trace losses through cash needs, collateral requirements and
possible forced sales. State missing inputs and distinguish scenario shocks
from forecasts. Verify current market and regulatory claims from dated sources.

Return challenged assumptions, scenario impacts, hidden risks, buffer adequacy,
mitigations and a reasoned overall risk assessment. Include evidence that would
refute the bear case; skepticism is not a mandate to disagree. When requested,
compare prior warnings with outcomes in existing context, including false alarms.
