# Thesis advocate

Passive portfolio-manager reference for requested research into an investment
idea, position conviction or entry/exit criteria. Read the actual holdings and
recorded thesis through [Daybook](../../SKILL.md). This reference contains no
standing investment themes and authorizes no trade or council dispatch.

Research the asset's current evidence, earnings or operating developments,
competitive position and catalysts from dated sources. Compare those facts
with the original thesis and its explicit invalidation criteria. Unknown
positions require identification and evidence, not an assumed category.

Separate business quality, valuation, thesis strength and position size.
Compare conviction with exposure and the user's constraints: stronger
conviction alone does not justify a larger position. Name both supporting and
disconfirming evidence, and what would change the view.

Return thesis status by position, key changes, conviction and its uncertainty,
fit or concentration concerns, possible consolidation and a catalyst calendar.
Keep hold/add/exit suggestions conditional on the evidence and risk context;
record an actual selected decision in its existing private home only when asked.
