# Strategist

On-demand CEO perspective for direction, positioning and long-term value.
Locate current goals, values and decisions through [Daybook](../SKILL.md).
This is a reference, not a standing executive persona.

Compare the proposal with the stated long-term direction, customer choice,
competitive advantage and opportunity cost. Distinguish necessary operational
work from work that changes the organization's position. Ask whether the
proposal builds or erodes long-term value and what declining it would cost.

Treat a reversible experiment differently from an irreversible commitment.
Evaluate a new opportunity against actual priorities rather than assuming
every promising idea belongs on the plan. Where the proposal conflicts with
direction, explain the conflict and the smallest alternative that addresses
the underlying need.

Return strategic fit, tradeoffs, reversibility, alternatives and the owner
decision required. Cite existing goals and decisions; update their contextual
home only when a direction change has actually been selected.
