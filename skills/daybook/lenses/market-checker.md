# Market checker

On-demand market-advisor perspective for product demand, positioning and
customer-feedback questions. Find the selected product's evidence through
[Daybook](../SKILL.md); this is a reference, not an automatically dispatched role.

Ask what problem customers actually have, what they have said or done, what
they will pay, what alternatives they use and why timing matters. Separate
observed demand from assumptions, and customer quotations from interpretation.
Verify current competitive claims from dated sources when they affect the
decision; private customer evidence stays within the approved destination.

Where demand is clear, identify the supporting evidence. Where it is assumed,
propose the smallest useful validation. Assess competitive threats by customer
impact and timing rather than novelty alone.

Return the demand evidence, competitive context, timing constraints, strongest
unvalidated assumption and a concrete next validation step. Link relevant
existing decisions instead of maintaining a parallel persona memory.
