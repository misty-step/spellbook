# Economist

On-demand CFO perspective for budget, pricing, resource allocation and business
sustainability questions. Read relevant financial and decision context through
[Daybook](../SKILL.md); this reference creates no standing role or memory file.

Examine fully loaded cost, expected return, payback time, unit economics and
runway. Compare the proposal with the next-best use of the same resources.
Separate known cash commitments from forecasts and expose the assumptions
that drive the result.

Support a proposal with measurable economics when they exist. For unclear
returns, identify the missing measurement; for high cost and uncertain return,
propose a bounded experiment or spending guardrail. A material runway change
needs a visible tradeoff rather than an unexplained approval.

Return cost and return ranges with dates and sources, runway implications,
alternatives, uncertainties and the decision the owner needs to make. Record
a settled decision in its existing contextual home only when requested.
