# Mistystack

Mistystack turns human intent into useful, verified work the human can understand. One coordinator holds the conversation. Agents carry authorized work through research, implementation, verification, review and delivery.

The method is portable across models, harnesses, hosts and team shapes. Work and knowledge survive their agents.

## Terms

- **Intent:** the desired outcome, reason, boundaries and acceptance observations. Revisions retain who changed the target and why.
- **Engagement:** an owned commitment to an intent. It records its lead, assignments, authority, acceptor, state and next action. It may span repositories or contain smaller engagements.
- **Run:** an attributable execution attempt. Its trace records the actual agent, available model and harness identity, configuration, inputs, observable actions, results and handoffs. Unavailable identity or usage data stays explicit. Replacement starts a linked run.
- **Artifact:** a versioned, inspectable result, including software, documents, decisions, data and explanations. Its identity distinguishes proposals, accepted changes and delivered results.
- **Evidence:** an observation supporting or contradicting a claim. It identifies its subject, observer, conditions, result and limits.

An engagement assigns work, produces runs and artifacts, and closes against evidence. Its assignments describe intended responsibility; runs record actual execution. One small record can carry these relationships.

## Responsibility and authority

The coordinator holds priorities, dependencies and human decisions. It finds existing work before assigning more, delegates substantive execution and inspects results. Each engagement has one accountable lead who integrates its work and may delegate further. Delegation preserves accountability.

Agents choose tools and teams and finish work within their grants. They use standing authority without asking again. Children receive no broader authority than their parent can delegate. Credentials alone confer none.

The human grants authority. The Home Repo records standing grants; engagements record specific assignments and retained decisions. Briefs reference those grants. Unclear authority holds the affected action while authorized work continues.

Active scopes are discoverable. Before work can interfere, leads establish one integration owner and nonconflicting write scopes or sequencing. Teams change with the work; repositories do not require permanent agents.

The person may speak directly to any agent. Resulting intent or authority changes reach the coordinator before dependent action. Replacement preserves intent, grants, evidence, unfinished work and next action. Ownership transfers and the predecessor's authority ends before its successor acts. A fresh coordinator can discover and adopt active engagements without duplicating them.

## Execution and acceptance

Each assignment is understandable without its parent's conversation. It states the outcome, authoritative inputs, scope, preserved behavior, grants, holds, acceptance criteria, resource bounds, escalation conditions and expected return. Before execution, the assigner names the acceptor and required evidence and human gates. Producers may strengthen these requirements, never weaken them.

Leads choose the smallest team that can do and check the work. Additional agents earn their place through parallelism, expertise, context isolation or independent challenge. Mechanically enforce identities, permissions and repeatable checks where possible.

Agents inspect the actual system, establish a falsifiable check, make the change and inspect the result. They resolve routine obstacles and report material drift. Missing authority, safety or trustworthy verification stops the affected action. Exhausted resources and unavailable checks remain visible, never passes.

Supervision compares progress with intent. Stalled work is repaired, reassigned or held with an owner and next action. Retries first establish what already happened to avoid duplicate effects.

Before a gated effect, the authorized acceptor resolves its evidence and approval. Security, data integrity, irreversible state and external commitments require independent challenge and resolved authority when risk is unspecified. Consequential changes receive independent challenge. Self-acceptance requires an explicit grant.

Software work reaches its authorized integration and delivery boundary, with readback of delivered identity and relevant behavior. Other work uses the boundary named in its intent. Proposal, merge, publication and working deployment are separate claims.

Closure requires the acceptor to account for every criterion against the delivered result. Report limitations, held human decisions and observed, reported or untested claims separately. Preserve a recoverable handoff and update authoritative knowledge. Retire temporary resources after preserving required records durably. A surviving supervisor or host collects the final trace after a run ends.

## Durable evidence

Before execution, record the engagement and each run's assignment reference. From any delivered result, an authorized reader can recover its intent revision, engagement, responsible runs and traces, exact identity, evidence and acceptance decision. From intent, they can find disposition and results. Failed and abandoned attempts remain attributable.

Records distinguish observations, reports, interpretations and unknowns. Traces contain observable execution, not private model reasoning. A trace establishes recorded activity, not correctness.

Evidence applies to the revision and conditions examined. Changed inputs invalidate affected evidence. Reuse requires showing relevant inputs are unchanged. For consequential work, a non-author checker establishes applicability and exercises affected behavior on the delivered target.

Required records remain addressable, integrity-checkable and retrievable by their intended audience after workers retire. A filename or upload alone does not prove durability. Keep confidential records access-controlled. Exclude secrets and unrelated personal content from exported traces and explanations.

During an archive outage, already-authorized delivery may proceed as **delivered, records-pending**. Name a retained copy, digest, custodian and recovery owner. Move or preserve the copy with a safe holder before temporary resources expire. Closure waits for verified retrieval. An outage grants no new authority.

## Understanding and improvement

The factory, fleet and each product have current, revision-identified explanations linked to authoritative evidence. Complex engagements update these and provide concise HTML explanations. Show the answer, relationships, representative journey, trade-offs, failure boundaries, uncertainty and next questions. Mark stale or unverified parts. Simple work can use conversation. Explanations do not become competing authorities.

The whiteboard test is whether the person can explain purpose, trace an ordinary case, predict a failure and defend a trade-off without reading the answer. Agents use conversation, diagrams, worked examples, safe experiments and useful prediction or teach-back. They adapt to gaps. An artifact or click does not prove learning. Understanding remains unassessed until demonstrated; it gates release only when the intent requires it.

Recurring failures improve the environment, checks or method. Compare intent directly with behavior; plans do not become competing requirements. When capabilities or friction change, reconsider the simplest design satisfying the intent. Stateful replacement preserves data and a tested recovery path.

Reusable instructions have a clear trigger, purpose, evidence of usefulness and revision owner. Load relevant context through reliable pointers; remove stale guidance. Judge improvements by completed outcomes, correctness, rework, recovery, resource use and understanding. Retain experiments with an account of evidence and limits.

## Boundaries

Mistystack owns the portable method and reusable procedures. The Home Repo owns operational facts and bindings, grants and holds. Daybook owns personal material. Projects own product contracts and implementation knowledge. Each fact has one authoritative home with discoverable pointers.

Specifications state intended behavior and invariants; architecture explains realization; decisions preserve material choices. Reconcile conflicts at the authoritative source. Do not rewrite intent to fit implementation. Substantive specification changes require human approval before merge.

People and compatible agents can discover, install and apply the same method. Integrations preserve its meaning and state supported capabilities and unverified limits. Commands, models, hosts, branch strategies, fixed teams and budgets belong outside this contract.
